    Highlights

    HDR400 Brightness & QHD Resolution

    This KTC H27E8 gaming monitor features a 27-inch 16:9 screen design with a 2560x1440 QHD display. Every image and text element appears crisp and sharp, eliminating blur entirely for an immersive entertainment experience. HDR400 brightness further enhances contrast between light and dark areas, delivering more vivid and lifelike visuals that let you savor every intricate detail.

    Restoring Authentic Details

    This H27E8 gaming monitor employs a fast IPS panel, delivering wide viewing angles and exceptional color accuracy for lifelike visuals. With 100% sRGB color gamut coverage, 16.7 million colors, and 400 cd/m² brightness, it presents vibrant colors and crisp image details, perfectly suited for diverse multimedia entertainment and office applications.

    Ultra-High 275Hz Refresh Rate with Adaptive Sync

    The H27E8 achieves smoother, clearer visuals and motion effects through its 275Hz overclocked refresh rate and 1ms MPRT response time, virtually eliminating input lag for an exceptionally fluid gaming experience. Compatible with FreeSync and G-Sync technologies, it eliminates screen tearing and stuttering, delivering seamless gameplay.

    Comfortable Viewing Angle

    The KTC H27E8 gaming monitor features an adjustable height stand supporting flexible tilt and swivel adjustments, helping you effortlessly find the optimal viewing angle. It also includes the anti-glare shield to effectively reduce screen glare or reflection interference. It also supports 100x100mm VESA mounting for easy wall mounting. The flicker-free design combined with the low blue light tech actively counteracts harmful blue light radiation, effectively alleviating visual fatigue during extended use.

    Flexible Connectivity

    This gaming monitor is equipped with dual HDMI 2.0 ports, dual DP 1.4 ports, and an audio out. It supports high resolutions and ultra-fast refresh rates for premium audio-visual transmission and effortlessly connects multiple devices simultaneously.
