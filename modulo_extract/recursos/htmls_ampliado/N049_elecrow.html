PiTower Gen 2 – Pi 5 Aluminum Mini PC Case

The PiTower Gen 2 is an integrated Mini PC chassis system specifically designed for the Raspberry Pi 5. Compared to the PiTower Gen 1, the Gen2 adds RGB lighting effects and features fan pads to significantly reduce fan noise. Leveraging the hardware characteristics of the Raspberry Pi 5, it employs an aluminum alloy passive cooling design and a semi-pre-assembled structure, providing a plug-and-play desktop experience to meet the needs of various scenarios including development, learning, home office, and creative projects.

Not sure which PiTower is right for you?  Read our [PiTower Gen 1 vs. Gen 2 Comparison Guide]  to find the perfect fit for your needs.

Note: Raspberry Pi is not included. Only the following Raspberry Pi versions are supported: Raspberry Pi OS (Trixie). Underlying System: Debian 13 (Trixie) Kernel Version: Linux 6.12

Feature

  • Premium Aluminum Alloy Case: A premium aluminum alloy case with transparent acrylic panels showcases your hardware in style. Measuring just 134.5 × 127 × 76 mm, it combines a compact footprint, durable construction, and efficient passive cooling with a technologically advanced look.
  • 2.8-inch System Monitoring Secondary Screen: Equipped with a 2.8-inch full-color TFT screen (320×240), it displays real-time information such as CPU usage/temperature, SSD usage, network status, and time, providing a comprehensive overview of the host's status.
  •  Dual M.2 SSD Expansion Slots: Supports dual M.2 SSD storage expansion (compatible with NVMe/SATA, sizes 2230/2242/2260/2280), allowing for flexible high-speed read/write and storage combinations.
  • Semi-Pre-assembled Structure, Beginner-Friendly: Customized structure and interface layout for Raspberry Pi 5, it comes pre-installed with a cooling module, adapter board, and cables, requiring only a few screws for assembly, significantly reducing the difficulty of DIY PC building.
  • Programmable RGB Lighting Effects: Built-in customizable RGB lighting module, supporting 11 preset lighting schemes, allowing you to switch ambient lighting effects as you like.
  • Abundant Interfaces, Plug and Play: Fully exposed Type-C power supply, HDMI video output, USB 3.0/USB 2.0, and GPIO interfaces, allowing for quick connection of peripherals, sensors, and display devices without additional adapters.

 

PiTower Gen 2 delivers a desktop-grade Mini PC experience that balances thermal performance, expandability, and aesthetics for developers, makers, and home users. Achieving "one device in hand, worry-free communication."
