LR2021 LoRa Module

LR2021 is a compact wireless communication module powered by Semtech’s 4th-generation LR2021 LoRa chip. It supports Sub-GHz, 2.4 GHz ISM, and S-band (1900–2200 MHz), enabling flexible terrestrial and satellite communication. With up to 2.6 Mbps FLRC, -141.5 dBm sensitivity, and +22 dBm Sub-GHz transmit power, LR2021 delivers reliable long-range and high-speed connectivity. Its single-antenna multi-band design reduces BOM cost and PCB space, while the 10×10 mm compact package makes integration easy. Supporting LoRaWAN, Amazon Sidewalk, Wi-SUN, W-MBUS, Z-Wave, Thread, and Zigbee, LR2021 is ideal for global IoT, smart metering, asset tracking, industrial automation, and satellite-enabled applications.

Feature

  • Fourth-generation LoRa technology: Utilizes the Semtech LR2021, offering approximately 4.5 dB improvement in Sub-GHz sensitivity and supporting up to 2.6 Mbps FLRC.
  • Multi-band support: Supports Sub-GHz (150–960 MHz), 2.4 GHz, and S-band (1900–2200 MHz), covering both terrestrial and satellite communications.
  • High-performance RF: Maximum +22 dBm transmit power in Sub-GHz and +12 dBm in 2.4 GHz, with sensitivity as low as -141.5 dBm.
  • Single-antenna design: Supports multi-band switching, eliminating the need for an external RF switch, reducing BOM costs and saving PCB space.
  • Low power consumption and long range: Sleep current as low as 470 nA, typical communication range of 6–8 km, and line-of-sight exceeding 15 km.
  • Multi-protocol compatibility: Supports LoRaWAN, Amazon Sidewalk, Wi-SUN, W-MBUS, Z-Wave, Thread, and Zigbee.
  • Compact and easy to integrate: Utilizes a 10×10 mm, 20-pin surface-mount stamp hole package, suitable for space-constrained embedded devices.
  • Industry-grade reliability: Supports operating temperatures from -40°C to +85°C and is FCC ID and CE (RED) certified.
  • FCC, CE certification
