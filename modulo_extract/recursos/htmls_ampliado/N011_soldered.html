    Description

    Transform your large-scale IoT projects with the Inkplate 10, a 9.7-inch grayscale e-Paper display boasting 1200 × 825 pixel resolution and ultra-low power consumption of just 22 µA in deep sleep mode. Powered by the versatile ESP32 microcontroller with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 4.0 BLE, it delivers months of battery life from a single Li-Ion charge, thanks to the onboard charger and Real-Time Clock (RTC). Ideal for open-source photo frames, smart home dashboards, or weather displays, its fast 1.61s full refresh and quick partial update support ensure reliable performance.

    Getting started is fast and easy with full support for Arduino (100% compatible with Adafruit GFX library) or MicroPython, allowing you to have your project up and running in a few minutes. Expand your setup with microSD card storage for images and data, plus additional available GPIO, I²C, SPI, and Qwiic/Qwiic-compatible connectors for seamless integration. Completely open-source hardware and software make customization straightforward for any application. Of course, you're not alone with our documentation and getting started guides.

    Designed for high-performance embedded development, the Inkplate 10 fully supports Espressif's official ESP-IDF framework. This native environment grants developers low-level control over the hardware, enabling precise memory management, optimized display rendering routines, and custom power profiling to take full advantage of the board's ultra-low deep sleep capabilities. Whether building home prototypes or commercial-grade e-paper applications, ESP-IDF provides the robust foundation needed to unlock the full potential of Inkplate's wireless and processing features. A USB-C to USB-C cable is included in the package, so you can start using it right out of the box.

    Choose from options: with e-Paper or with e-Paper enclosure and battery for portable power and a beautiful protective enclosure. Have your projects run for months, even a year on a single charge! The Inkplate 10 measures 224.3 × 163 × 10 mm without EPD or 240 × 179.5 × 13.5 mm with enclosure. It's an awesome choice to bring your e-Paper project to life, today!

    Technical Specifications

    • Display Size: 9.7" e-paper (grayscale)
    • Resolution: 1200 × 825 pixels
    • Refresh Time: 1.61s (full), supports partial updates
    • Microcontroller: ESP32 (Wi-Fi + Bluetooth 4.0 BLE)
    • Power Supply: USB or Li-Ion battery (charger onboard)
    • Power Consumption: 22 µA in deep sleep mode
    • Programming Support: Arduino library, MicroPython
    • Storage: microSD card slot for image/data loading
    • Clock: Real-Time Clock (RTC) with battery holder
    • Expansion: GPIO, I²C, SPI, Qwiic/Qwiic-compatible
    • Open Source: Hardware and software
    • Dimensions (with e-Paper): 224.3 × 163 × 10 mm / 8.8 × 6.4 × 0.4 inch
    • Dimensions (with e-Paper, enclosure and battery): 240 × 179.5 × 13.5 mm / 9.4 × 7.1 × 0.5 inch
